*oruxmaps is a GPS tracking and navigation app for Windows that helps outdoor users plan a route, follow it, and log the trip as it happens.*

Most people who look for the oruxmaps app on PC are trying to solve the same problem: they want one tool that handles route planning, live tracking, and offline maps together, instead of juggling several apps that each do part of the job. oruxmaps is built to cover all three in a single, focused workspace.

## Why people use it
- `[Tracking]` Logs GPS tracks with distance, time, and elevation as you move
- `[Offline]` Keeps downloaded maps usable with no connection at all
- `[Waypoints]` Lets you mark and label points of interest along a route
- `[Sources]` Works with a range of online and offline map sources
- `[Export]` Saves tracks in formats other tools can open

## What it needs
- [ ] Windows 10 or later, 64-bit
- [ ] A standard modern processor and the RAM Windows itself calls for
- [ ] Extra free storage set aside for any map areas you download

## Setting up
1. Download the installer using the oruxmaps for PC button above.
2. Run it and step through the setup screens.
3. Launch oruxmaps once the installer finishes.

## Getting going
Once oruxmaps is open, pick a map source, choose the area you'll be traveling through, and download it while you still have a connection. From there, starting a new track is a matter of tapping record before you set out and stopping it when you're done.
